Apellis Pharmaceuticals: FP&A Team Analysis

Team consulting project · 5-person team

View presentation

Worked with Apellis's VP and Director of FP&A to diagnose why cross-department communication breakdowns were causing forecast delays and repeated revisions. We gathered evidence through leader interviews, surveys, workflow reviews, and analysis of email timing and revision trends, then recommended an emotional intelligence program and a structured team-norms system to move the group from "storming" to "norming."

  • FP&A
  • Interviews & surveys
  • Team effectiveness
  • Recommendations

Ethical Sourcing and Supplier Auditing

Group research presentation · 5-person team

View presentation

Researched how companies keep their supply chains ethical, from cultural and transparency challenges (the 1998 Sialkot soccer ball case and the 2013 Rana Plaza collapse) to certifications like SA8000, Fair Trade, and WRAP and the Sedex/SMETA audit method. We compared programs at Starbucks, IKEA, and Unilever and recommended practices for building long-term, accountable supplier relationships.

  • Supply chain
  • ESG & compliance
  • Case research

Kalshi Brand Direction

Brand strategy and design system

View deck

Built a complete brand direction for Kalshi, a prediction market, around the idea of making uncertainty look measurable. The deck defines the color palette, typography, layout grid, and photography rules, then applies them in three campaign key visuals (weather, economics, and science) and closes with a review of where the system drifted and how it was corrected.

  • Brand identity
